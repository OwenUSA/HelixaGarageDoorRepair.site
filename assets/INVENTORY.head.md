## How the geometry was taken

`assets.mjs` walks `<img>` (including `currentSrc` after `srcset` resolution), `<source>`,
CSS `url()` on **every** element, `<link rel=preload>`, `@font-face` sources and
video/poster, at 390 / 430 / 768 / 1440 against the locally-served reference. The
"highest resolution actually served" is the `natural` size of the `currentSrc` the browser
picked at the widest breakpoint, not the largest entry in the `srcset` attribute — those
differ wherever the theme's `sizes` attribute caps the choice.

WordPress srcset variants (`House6-300x200-1.png`, `…-980x574.png`) collapse to one slot.
The Responsive Lightbox full-size twins do **not**: they render at `0×0` and would otherwise
hand a 150×150 thumbnail slot a zero-area geometry.
